
# Restaurant Food Ordering Website

A restaurant website designed to provide customers with a convenient food browsing and ordering experience. Users can explore the restaurant menu, browse food categories, view promotional offers, manage their shopping cart, and place orders.

## Features

- Restaurant homepage
- Food menu browsing
- Food categories
- Promotional offers
- Menu item filtering
- Shopping cart management
- Add and remove cart items
- Update item quantities
- Order summary
- Customer contact information
- Order placement
- Responsive design for mobile, tablet, and desktop
